Launch & Monitor

Launching is the easy click. The discipline is in the two weeks after: not touching what needs time, and not ignoring what needs fixing. Five minutes a week on the right numbers beats an hour a day on the wrong ones.

Pre-launch check ten minutes that save a month
  • Click your own ads' paths end to end.Landing page on a phone → WhatsApp button → does the message arrive? Form → does the notification reach you? A broken step here means paying for leads you never see.
  • Verify conversions fire.Google Tag Assistant / Meta test events. "It's probably tracking" is how budgets die quietly.
  • Confirm daily budgets and end dates.Both platforms default to "run forever". Set a calendar reminder for the 4-week review before you launch, not after.
The numbers that matter ignore everything else, especially "impressions"
MetricWhat it tells you
Cost per enquiry G MTotal spend ÷ conversations started. The only number that matters weekly. Judge it against what a student is worth: monthly fee × months they typically stay.
Enquiry → trial → student rateHappens in WhatsApp, not in the ad platform — track it in a simple sheet. If enquiries come but don't convert, the ads are fine and the follow-up isn't.
Search terms report GWhat people actually typed. Weekly 5-minute read; move irrelevant terms to negatives. This is where Google budgets leak.
Frequency MAbove ~3–4, the same people keep seeing the same ad — refresh the creative or tighten the budget.
CTR / CPCDiagnostics only. Bad CTR = message problem. Fine CTR + no enquiries = landing page problem. They locate the leak; they are not the goal.
Days 1–3

Look, don't touch. Confirm delivery, spend, and that test enquiries arrive. No settings changes — both platforms are in learning mode.

Weekly · 5 min

Dashboard + search terms. Cost per enquiry, new negatives, reply-time check. One change per week maximum, and note what you changed.

Week 4 · 30 min

Verdict per channel. Cost per student vs student value. Keep, fix, or kill — with numbers, not impressions of impressions.

The discipline rules Never judge a campaign in its first week. Never change two things at once — you won't know which one worked. Never scale budget more than ~20% at a time. And when a campaign works, the boring move — leaving it alone — is usually the right one.
